# Features

- Dictate or type a study, confirm an editable card and track outstanding honoraria by hospital.
- Record individual or grouped payments and review production and payment history.
- Track a patient's invoice request or a hospital invoice for selected studies. A study can belong to one invoice. The payer, requester and invoice recipient can differ.
- Invoice badges show requested, issued or sent status. The Facturas view groups studies by invoice and puts outstanding requests first. An optional folio or UUID identifies the externally issued document.
- Invoice writes require connectivity. Older studies can be invoiced without migration. An invoice remains linked when a payment is undone.
