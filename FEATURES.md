# Features

- Dictate or type a study, confirm an editable card and track outstanding honoraria by hospital.
- Record individual or grouped payments and review production and payment history.
- Track a patient's invoice request or a hospital invoice for selected studies. A study can belong to one invoice. The payer, requester and invoice recipient can differ.
- Invoice badges show requested, issued or sent status. The Facturas view groups studies by invoice and puts outstanding requests first. An optional folio or UUID identifies the externally issued document.
- Invoice writes require connectivity. Older studies can be invoiced without migration. An invoice remains linked when a payment is undone.

The private invoice editor stores a delivery email for individual and hospital invoices. Manual invoice entry can omit the address. The planned public QR form is for requests with email delivery and requires an address.

Facturas lists hospitals with eligible studies. Start from a hospital, select studies and use their total or enter a different amount. Save or update reusable billing details explicitly. Without a saved profile, the editor can reuse data from the latest issued hospital invoice. Copy the draft and open the official SAT service for manual completion.

The main navigation links directly to Facturas. Settings shows the release number. Hospital drafts can calculate the gross base for an exempt invoice using an explicitly selected ISR rate.

Save hospital defaults directly from the draft using Guardar solo perfil, without creating an invoice.

The patient invoice preview includes a translucent blue panel, replaceable decor and client-side field validation.

The preview shows the MRI in a navy banner above a white form. Regime and usage are selected by code and name.

A full fiscal address may be entered optionally.

Full fiscal address is required on the preview. Facturas displays the local test folio.

The intake requires a positive MXN amount with up to two decimals and displays it on the local test card.

The invoice preview requires the reported form of payment and displays it beside the amount.

Email-first form, optional Google account and optional profile retention. Amount and payment are captured for every request.

Google sign-in reveals a clearly labeled fiscal profile opt-in.

Google sign-in has an immediate activity indicator, a prompt during longer waits, and a fiscal profile lookup message.
